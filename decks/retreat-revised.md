# Students Will Inherit a Practice, Not a Bot

### In my Fall class, each team will ***branch*** Peter's Office Hours into its own version.

- Add the team's design and development documents, vocabulary, research, references, and examples
- Keep meeting notes, schedules, and responsibilities in the shared workspace
- Record build and iteration histories and playtest observations
- Define what the agent should and should not do
- Preserve decisions, open questions, disagreements, and dissent
- Improve the shared corpus as the project develops
- Keep consequential creative choices with the students
